# Falcon Translines Ltd. - Project Brief

## Company Name
FALCON TRANSLINES LTD.
*Falcon Translines Ltd. is a Canadian transportation and freight carrier based in Quebec, providing reliable trucking and freight transportation services across Canada.*

## Our Fleet
We provide transportation using different types of equipment to accommodate different shipment requirements:
- Box Trucks
- Dry Vans
- Refrigerated Trucks / Reefers
- Sprinter Vans
*Our fleet allows us to handle different freight sizes, shipment requirements, and delivery timelines.*

## Our Transportation Services
Falcon Translines Ltd. provides:
- Expedited Freight Transportation
- Time-Sensitive Shipments
- Long-Distance Transportation
- Local Delivery Services
- Dedicated Transportation
- General Freight Transportation
- Full Truckload Transportation
- Regional and Nationwide Freight Services

*We handle both local and long-distance shipments and can provide transportation solutions based on the customer's shipment requirements.*

## What We Want Customers to Understand
The website should communicate that Falcon Translines Ltd. is:
- Reliable
- Professional
- Responsive
- Flexible
- Experienced in freight transportation
- Equipped with multiple types of trucks
- Capable of handling both local and long-distance shipments
- Available for expedited and time-sensitive transportation

## Website Copy Style
- Keep the language professional, straightforward, and trustworthy.
- Do not use exaggerated claims such as "Canada's #1 trucking company," "the largest carrier," or other claims that cannot be verified.
- Do not make the company sound like a freight brokerage if the page is presenting Falcon Translines Ltd. as the carrier.
- Focus on trucking, transportation, freight, fleet, reliability, flexibility, and nationwide Canadian coverage.

## Main Calls to Action
Include clear calls to action throughout the website, such as:
- Get a Quote
- Request Transportation
- Book a Truck
- Contact Us
- Request a Shipment

## Design Instructions
- Recreate the general layout, spacing, typography, section structure, visual hierarchy, navigation style, button placement, and professional appearance from the reference images.
- fully responsive for Desktop, Laptop, Tablet, Mobile.
- Content and sections modular and easy to edit later.
